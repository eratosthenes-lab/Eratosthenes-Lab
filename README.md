# Matteo Putzulu — Eratosthenes Lab

**Civil and Industrial Engineer (M.Eng.) | Professional Engineer | International Welding Engineer (IWE) | Data Scientist**

I am a civil and industrial engineer and a data scientist (mainly Python and R). I hold a Bachelor of Engineering degree (B.Eng) in civil engineering (3 years), followed by a Master’s degree (M.Eng) in transport systems engineering (2 years) from the University of Bologna.
I have taken individual university courses—including final exams—within the departments of industrial and environmental engineering at the University of Bologna and the University of Cagliari.
I obtained the IWE Qualification Diploma (1 years) provided by IIS–IWI of Genoa and EWF–CWB Group. I completed the Master in Advanced Welding Engineering (1 years) at the Department of Mechanical Engineering of the University of Genoa.
My primary interests concern transport engineering, system navigations (GNSS and INS), environment and energy (fossil and renewable) engineering, and materials engineering (metallic, non-metallic, and composite).

Welcome to **Eratosthenes-Lab**, my personal workspace dedicated to quantitative analysis, statistical modeling, engineering calculations, and data science.

### Focus Areas
* **Statistical & Econometric Analysis:** Time series, regression diagnostics, and residual analysis.
* **Data Science & Engineering:** Data-driven modeling applied to civil, industrial, energy, and environmental systems.
* **Reproducible Research:** Open notebooks and scripts supporting technical articles and publications.
